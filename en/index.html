1. About the service

eduroam (education roaming) is a Wi-Fi authentication roaming service operated by universities and research institutions worldwide. Members of a participating institution connect at any other participating site using the account issued by their own institution, without applying for a guest account.

Example Research Institute is a participating institution of eduroam Korea and provides the SSID eduroam. Once a device is configured, it works at every eduroam site in Korea and abroad without further changes.

Authentication is performed by the authentication server of your home institution, not by the site you are visiting. Your credentials are therefore never passed to the visited institution, which receives only the result of the authentication.

2. How to use

2.1. Visitors

This applies to members of another eduroam participating institution who are visiting Example Research Institute.

Who Members of eduroam participating institutions
How
  1. Select the network name (SSID) eduroam from the Wi-Fi list.
  2. Enter your home institution account in the form username@your-institution-domain. The domain part is required.
  3. Once configured, the same settings work at every eduroam site worldwide, including Example Research Institute.
Support The Wi-Fi support desk of your home institution

Setup by operating system

Windows
  1. Download and install the institution CA certificate. Double-click the file, choose Install Certificate → store location Current User → Place all certificates in the following store → Trusted Root Certification Authorities.
  2. Select eduroam from the Wi-Fi list in the taskbar and connect.
  3. Enter username@example.re.kr and your password.
  4. When the certificate prompt appears, choose Show certificate details and confirm that the certificate name is rad.eduroam.example.re.kr and that the thumbprint matches the value in Certificate details above, then continue.
macOS, iOS, iPadOS
  1. On macOS, download the institution CA certificate and double-click it to add it to the login keychain in Keychain Access. Double-click the added certificate, expand Trust and set When using this certificate to Always Trust. You will be asked for an administrator password.
  2. On iOS and iPadOS, download the certificate in Safari, install the profile under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, then enable it under Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Without this last step the certificate is not trusted.
  3. Select eduroam from the Wi-Fi list.
  4. Enter username@example.re.kr and your password.
  5. On the certificate screen, confirm the certificate name is rad.eduroam.example.re.kr, open Show Details and check that the fingerprint matches the value in Certificate details above, then choose Trust.
Android
  1. Download the institution CA certificate and install it under Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate. Install it even if a warning appears. Menu names differ by vendor and version (Samsung: Settings → Security and privacy → Other security settings → Install from device storage).
  2. Select eduroam from the Wi-Fi list.
  3. Set EAP method to PEAP and phase 2 authentication to MSCHAPV2.
  4. Set CA certificate to the institution CA certificate you installed and enter rad.eduroam.example.re.kr as the domain. Choose Use system certificates only if your institution uses a publicly trusted certificate.
  5. Enter username@example.re.kr as the identity, anonymous@example.re.kr as the anonymous identity, and your password.

⚠️ On Android 11 and later the connection fails if the domain field is left empty. Android does not show a certificate prompt; it validates the server automatically against the CA certificate and domain you entered, so entering those two values correctly is the certificate check.

4. Security policy

When using eduroam at Example Research Institute you must comply with our information security policy. When visiting another institution, the policy of that institution applies.

Service restrictions

  • Internet bandwidth on the Example Research Institute eduroam service is limited.
  • Certain management ports are blocked for security reasons, including ports used for remote access, file sharing and mail submission.
  • If a blocked port is required for your work, please request an exception through the contact below.

Account use

  • Accounts are for the sole use of the account holder and must not be transferred or shared.
  • If you believe your account has been compromised, change the password immediately and notify the contact below.
  • The account holder is responsible for any incident arising from shared use of the account.

Prohibited activity and enforcement

Where the following activity is identified, access is blocked without prior notice. Depending on the case, the user's home institution is notified and the matter is handled under the applicable laws of the Republic of Korea.

  • Transmission of material that infringes copyright or other rights of third parties
  • Unauthorised access to information networks, vulnerability scanning, and denial-of-service attacks
  • Distribution of malware and sending of unsolicited bulk mail
  • Distribution of unlawful information and other activity in breach of applicable law
  • Any activity that disrupts the normal operation of the network

Connection logs

eduroam records connection logs in order to perform authentication. Logs are retained by both the home institution and the visited institution, and are used only for fault handling and security incident response. Retention periods and handling are governed by the privacy policy of each institution.
